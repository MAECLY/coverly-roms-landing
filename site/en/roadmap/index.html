What's done and what's next.

Each version, with its date and what it brought. Below, what's next, in the order it will be built and with its status.

Shipped

Each shelf is a published version; the last one has no number yet.

v0.1.0 17 October 2026

  • Audit: what each system is missing, exportable to CSV or JSON
  • Fill covers, screenshots and title screens, with a dry run and undo
  • Identification by CRC32, MD5 or SHA1, and a review queue for doubtful matches
  • Eight handheld and frontend layouts, and your own
  • Library browser and a panel for each game
  • SteamGridDB with your own key
  • Sorting a downloads folder by system
  • Signed updates, with stable and beta channels
  • The coverly command line

v0.2.0 25 October 2026

  • Six library views and an icon for each system
  • Sorting with archives of every kind, RAR too
  • Disc games extracted from archives, each track checked
  • Duplicates set aside in _Duplicados/ or the Trash, with undo
  • coverly dedupe in the terminal

v0.3.0 2 November 2026

  • 131 systems, up from 54
  • PS4, PS5, Xbox One, Switch and 3DS recognised without keys
  • Switch names from a public catalogue, if you turn it on
  • The cover a game carries, when no source has one
  • Games that are a folder, inside zip and 7z too
  • Asks before writing a game of 10 GB or more
  • Loose multi-disc games grouped with their .m3u
  • Duplicates by title ID and version; on Wii U, the .wua stays over its folder
  • RetroArch finds the covers of titles with “&” or “:”
  • Credits: 62 sources and libraries with their licences
  • Downloads named by system and chip, and Windows ARM64
  • Organise and Duplicates screens redone, with their own settings

What's next

In priority order and without dates: each block starts when the app's maker decides.

  1. Windows ARM64, tested on real hardware

    The ARM64 installers are already built. They still need a test on real hardware, and so does an update from 0.2.0.

    Done, being tested3 of 5 tasks

  2. The last systems

    All 131 systems are in the code. Two arcade boards and a few folder names are still to be decided.

    Almost there65 of 69 tasks

  3. Updates and DLC inside their game

    Each game in its own folder, with its updates, DLC, demos and revisions, and its cover in all six views.

    Being researched0 of 15 tasks

  4. Your library

    A library that lives on your computer, not just the folder you opened: collections, a page for each game and art kept per game.

    Waits for: Updates and DLC inside their game.

    Waiting on another block0 of 32 tasks

  5. Names and art by title ID

    Public sources for the games cover databases lack: the name and the art, looked up by title ID.

    Being researched1 of 28 tasks

  6. Sync with your handhelds

    See which games are in the library, which are on each handheld and which only on one of them, and move them across.

    Waits for: Updates and DLC inside their game, Your library.

    Waiting on another block0 of 20 tasks

  7. Saved games

    Find, sort and back up your saves, each one next to its game.

    Waits for: Updates and DLC inside their game, Your library.

    Waiting on another block0 of 17 tasks

  8. More frontends

    Sort and export the library for frontends on Windows, Linux, macOS, Android and iOS, with art where each one looks for it.

    Planned1 of 18 tasks

  9. Credits

    The tab that credits every source, database and library is done. The credits of the sources that come with names and art by title ID are still to add.

    Almost there7 of 8 tasks

Each block's detail is in the app's TODO.md, and what shipped, in its changelog.
