Covers and art

Why Coverly exists, and where it shows most.

Audit
Counts, per system, which games lack a cover, screenshot or title screen. Read-only, and exports to CSV or JSON.
v0.1.0
Fingerprint identification
CRC32, MD5 or SHA1 against libretro-database, or else the name. Arcade short names through the MAME and FBNeo DATs.
v0.1.0
Dry run and undo
Nothing is written until you ask. Backups go to the app's data folder, and Undo puts the card back as it was.
v0.1.0
Review queue
Doubtful matches wait in a queue you walk with the keyboard, and each says why it was picked.
v0.1.0
Region and source order
It prefers the ROM's region, your source order or SteamGridDB's votes.
v0.1.0
SteamGridDB with your key
Optional. The key is kept in the system keychain.
v0.1.0
The game's own cover
If no source has one, it uses the icon the game carries on PS3, PS Vita, Wii U, 3DS and Xbox 360. On PS4, PS5, Xbox One and Xbox Series it tries that first.
v0.3.0
Names RetroArch finds
Titles with “&” or “:” are saved the way RetroArch looks for them.
v0.3.0
Change it by hand
From the game's panel you change, search, remove or use your own image.
v0.1.0
Clean images
They are re-encoded, so they carry no metadata, and written one at a time.
v0.1.0
Pace and time left
While it fills, you see covers per minute and what's left.
v0.3.0

Handhelds and layouts

Eight layouts and 131 systems, each with its folder.

Eight layouts, detected
Anbernic stock, GarlicOS, Onion OS, MinUI and NextUI, muOS, ES-DE, Batocera, Knulli and EmulationStation, and RetroArch.
v0.1.0
Your own layout
For other firmware, path templates with a live preview.
v0.1.0
gamelist.xml
On Batocera, Knulli and EmulationStation it updates it, with undo.
v0.1.0
131 systems
Consoles, handhelds, computers, arcade and engines, each with the folder every layout uses.
v0.3.0
An icon for every system
In the sidebar, the filters, the game panel and the reports.
v0.2.0
Names the card accepts
File names that work on FAT and exFAT.
v0.1.0

Sort by system

From a downloads folder to a tidy library.

Sort a folder
Moves or copies each game into its system folder, never overwriting, with a dry run, resume and undo.
v0.1.0
Archives by their contents
zip, 7z, tar, gz, bz2, xz, zst and lz4; RAR through unrar, 7-Zip or bsdtar.
v0.2.0
Disc games, extracted
Checking the size and CRC of every track. If it stops, you resume it.
v0.2.0
Games that are a folder
PS3, Vita, Wii U, PS4, PS5, Xbox and others move as one item, from inside a zip or 7z too.
v0.3.0
Multi-disc with its .m3u
Loose discs of one game are grouped, with a note when one is missing.
v0.3.0
Asks before 10 GB
“Yes / Yes to all / No”, with the size and the space left.
v0.3.0
Updates and DLC with their game
Listed under their game, not as games of their own.
v0.3.0
Current consoles, no keys
Switch, 3DS, PS4, PS5, Xbox One and Xbox Series are recognised from what's stored in the clear; anything unreadable stays “Encrypted”.
v0.3.0
Switch name catalogue
Optional and off. One region and language from blawar/titledb, at most once a week.
v0.3.0

Duplicates

By contents, never by name, and nothing deleted.

By contents
Exact copies, the same disc in another format and, if you ask, one ROM per game.
v0.2.0
Rules for the one that stays
You reorder them or turn them off, and every pick says why.
v0.2.0
To _Duplicados/ or the Trash
With their saves, states and art. Nothing is deleted, and Undo brings it back.
v0.2.0
Different saves, untouched
If two copies have different saves, it touches neither.
v0.2.0
By title ID and version
Of two versions of one game it suggests the newer, and an update is never a copy of its game. On Wii U, the .wua stays over its folder.
v0.3.0
Each copy, in full
Region, size, format, cover and a button to show it in the Finder or Explorer.
v0.3.0

Library and views

Six ways to look at what you have.

Six views
Covers, Details, Columns, Gallery, Art slots and Shelves, on ⌘/Ctrl+1 to 6.
v0.2.0
One keyboard model
Arrows, Home and End, type to jump and Space for Quick look, in every view.
v0.2.0
Each library, its view
It remembers the view and its options for the 20 latest libraries.
v0.2.0
View options
Cover size, grouping, sorting, columns and the inspector.
v0.2.0
Big libraries
More than 10,000 games without waiting.
v0.1.0

Command line

All of the above, scriptable.

$ coverly --help
Audit and fill ROM cover art

Commands:
  audit     Report which ROMs lack cover, screenshot or title art
  catalog   Manage the optional name catalogues
  dedupe    Find the same game stored more than once
  fill      Fill missing art from the enabled sources
  organize  Sort a pile of loose ROMs into one folder per system
  profiles  List the layout profiles
  systems   List the known systems
  undo      Undo the last job
  update    Check for a newer coverly, or install it
Dry run until --apply
fill, organize and dedupe write nothing without --apply.
v0.1.0
Reports as JSON
Every report comes out as JSON if you ask, ready for a script.
v0.1.0
dedupe
Sets duplicates aside from the terminal.
v0.2.0
catalog switch
Downloads, checks or removes the Switch name catalogue.
v0.3.0
--skip-large
Leaves games of 10 GB or more where they are, without asking.
v0.3.0
A single binary
For macOS, Windows and Linux, x64 and ARM64.
v0.3.0

Settings and trust

No account, no telemetry, signed versions and credits in plain sight.

No account, no telemetry
It only talks to the art sources and, unless you turn it off, the update check.
v0.1.0
Signed versions
Verified with minisign before they install; stable and beta channels, and it never restarts mid-job.
v0.1.0
Credits
62 sources, databases and libraries, with their licence and the credit they ask for.
v0.3.0
Settings in tabs
Organise and Duplicates in their own tabs, advanced options folded, and search with ⌘F or Ctrl+F.
v0.3.0
One download per chip
Macs with Apple silicon or Intel, and Windows ARM64.
v0.3.0
Spanish and English
Light, dark or the system's.
v0.1.0
